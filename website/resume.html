Robotics projects

Pedestrian crossing-intent prediction from pose, gesture and scene context

Predicts whether a pedestrian will step onto the road 1.5 seconds before they do, using body pose and the scene around them. Result: 0.633 AP on real video (pose alone: 0.385).

Multi-agent reinforcement learning with intent inference at unsignalised intersections

Two self-driving cars learn to take turns at a junction with no lights or radio, by reading each other's motion. Result: 12 of 12 seeds solved, 0 collisions.

Demo-quality ablations for a vision-language-action model (SmolVLA) on LIBERO-10

Scores every teleoperated demo for how well it was driven, then trains a robot-arm policy on the best, worst and random halves. Result: 41.6% vs 40.0% vs 39.8% task success.

Privileged-learning transformer policy for rough-terrain quadruped locomotion

A transformer policy that walks a four-legged robot over slopes and stairs using only what its joints feel. No camera, no lidar. Result: Teacher-student pipeline, 61 tests passing.

Perception and pull-direction selection for robotic sorting of pipe piles

An industry project: deciding which pipe a robot should pull from a jumbled pile, and in which direction. Result: Industry project, ongoing.

FP16 and INT8 TensorRT optimisation of YOLO11 for real-time road-user detection

Runs YOLO11 detection on TensorRT at three precisions and measures accuracy, speed, memory and energy for each. Result: 574 FPS, 3.57x faster, -0.09 mAP.

World-model reinforcement learning for traffic-scene anticipation

A small DreamerV3-style world model, written from scratch, that imagines a traffic scene 15 steps ahead to test manoeuvres before doing them. Result: 4x more accurate than the baseline.

In-cabin speech and gesture grounding for driver-vehicle interaction

Turns what a driver says and points at into an action, and asks a question instead of guessing when the two don't agree. Result: Silent wrong actions: 37.5% to 0%.

Reproduction and ablation of TITAN action priors for pedestrian trajectory forecasting

Reimplemented TITAN (CVPR 2020) from the paper alone, with every prior on its own switch so the ablation can be run row by row. Result: Within 10% of published baselines.
